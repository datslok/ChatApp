ChatApp for Windows

The chat in its own window, with notifications. It keeps itself up to date.

Download for Windows

Installing

  1. Open the file you downloaded (ChatApp_…_x64-setup.exe).
  2. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC": the app isn't signed by a company. Click More info, then Run anyway. That's only needed once: after that, the app updates itself.
  3. Log in with your usual account.

ChatApp for Android

The chat as an app on your phone, with notifications and calls. It keeps itself up to date.

Download for Android

Installing

  1. Your browser may ask whether to keep the file ("File might be harmful"): it says that about every app that doesn't come from the Play Store. Tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded file (ChatApp-android-….apk). Android asks whether your browser may install apps: Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then back.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect says it doesn't know the app, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  4. Open ChatApp and log in with your usual account. Updates come by themselves.
