What it can do

💬

Messages

Channels and DMs, with replies, reactions, custom emoji, @mentions, formatting and link previews.

🔊

Voice & video

A voice channel and calls in DMs, with cameras, AI noise suppression and a volume per person.

🖥️

Screen sharing

Share your screen or a game at up to 1440p and 60 fps, with sound, encoded on your graphics card.

👥

Friends & profiles

Friend requests, who's online, profile pictures and banners (GIFs too), a status and a bio.

🔔

Notifications

For DMs and mentions, with sounds and unread counts, and muting for when you need quiet.

📲

Apps

For Windows and Android, with real notifications, and they keep themselves up to date. Or just use it in your browser.

Or just use ChatApp in your browser. On an iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.

Host it yourself

ChatApp is free and open source. It's one Python program with a SQLite database, with voice and video through LiveKit, and it runs on an ordinary PC at home, reachable through a Cloudflare Tunnel. No cloud servers, and your messages stay with you.
