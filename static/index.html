      Outbound packet loss:

      Inbound packet loss:

      The ping is the time to our voice server and back. You may notice delayed audio at 250 ms or higher, and sound robotic with more than 10% outbound packet loss. Inbound loss is what reaches you: if others sound robotic and it's high, the problem is on your side. TCP is a fallback when UDP is blocked, and handles lost packets worse. A screen share "held back by the CPU" needs a lower quality (or the graphics card encoder); "by the connection" means your upload can't keep up. Pauses: moments with no new frames, normal when the shared screen isn't changing; a problem only while it's moving.
